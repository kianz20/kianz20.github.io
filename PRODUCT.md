## Platform

## Users

Three audiences of equal standing, confirmed by Kian on 2026-11-05:

- **Employers and engineers** judging how Kian builds and thinks.
- **Prospective clients** who may want a site or platform built.
- **Curious peers** who arrive through GitHub, a game night, or a car forum.

The site has no sales goal. It shows the work and the person behind it.

## Product Purpose

The personal site of Kian Jazayeri, a full stack engineer at Sandfield in Auckland, New Zealand. It replaces a January 2024 page with a current record of Kian's personal projects. Success means a visitor leaves knowing what Kian has built, how Kian approaches it, and how to reach Kian.

## Product Principles

1. Show the real thing. Real screenshots and real mechanisms beat adjectives.
2. Every claim must be checkable against a repo, a merged PR, or a package page.
3. Be honest about status: live, private source, or not yet launched.
4. Each project gets the specific detail that makes it interesting, rather than a list of technologies.
5. Let personality show. Kian builds PCs, plays games, and likes cars.
